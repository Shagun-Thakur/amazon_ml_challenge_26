## Methodological Note

Positive coverage comes from the Cell 11 sampled true-positive
pairs.

Hard-negative activation comes from the deliberately difficult
negative population constructed in Cell 6.

The hard-negative population is a diagnostic stress-test set,
not the true competition class distribution. Therefore these
activation rates should not be interpreted as competition
precision.

The purpose is to determine which deterministic signals are
safe enough for candidate generation and which signals require
additional evidence from other fields.
