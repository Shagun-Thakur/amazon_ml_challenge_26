## 8. Interpretation


The deterministic rules are evaluated only for true-positive
coverage. High coverage does not imply safe matching because
multiple businesses can satisfy the same rule. False-positive
risk must therefore be evaluated against the hard-negative
population before any rule is used as a final matching decision.

The recall gap after deterministic structural rules is the key
evidence used to decide whether additional retrieval methods
such as TF-IDF, hashing, approximate nearest-neighbor retrieval,
or lightweight semantic retrieval are required.
